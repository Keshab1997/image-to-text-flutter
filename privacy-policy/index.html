Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026
App: SnapText OCR (com.keshabstudios.snaptext)
Operator: Keshab Studios
Contact: keshabstudios@gmail.com

This policy describes how the SnapText OCR Android app handles information. It is a product-specific notice, not legal advice. Please review it against the released app and your legal obligations before relying on it.

At a glance

SnapText OCR does not require an account and is not connected to an operator-run account, sync, or OCR server. OCR is performed on the device. The app saves preferences and (if enabled by use) recent scan text in local app storage. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Information can leave the device if you choose to share text, contact support, or use platform services.

Information the app handles

  • Images you choose or capture: The app uses the Android image picker and camera permission when you take a photo. The selected image is processed on your device for text recognition and cropping. Temporary image files may be created locally while the app works with an image.
  • Recognized and edited text: Text is produced on-device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition. The current OCR scripts are Latin, Chinese, Devanagari, Japanese, and Korean; Bengali OCR is not currently supported. You can edit, copy, clear, or share recognized text. The app does not send the image or recognized text to a server operated by Keshab Studios.
  • Scan history: Recent non-empty scans may be stored locally on your device. The history is limited to 50 entries; adding a repeated text replaces the older matching entry. You can delete entries or clear the history in the app.
  • App preferences: Theme and language choices are stored locally so they can be restored when you reopen the app.
  • Camera permission: Android asks for camera access only when you choose the camera feature. You can deny or revoke it in system settings; camera capture will then be unavailable.

The app does not ask you to create an account. It has no operator-run server, database, cloud backup, advertising, or analytics service. The app and operating system may require network access for platform functionality or service components; the app does not use that access to upload your selected image or OCR text to Keshab Studios.

Sharing and other apps

If you tap Share, the operating system opens the share interface and you choose whether and where to send text. The recipient app or service then handles the information under its own terms and privacy policy. Copying text places it on the device clipboard, which may be accessible to the operating system or other permitted applications.

If you email support, Keshab Studios receives the email address and any content you include. Do not include sensitive information unless it is needed to resolve your request. Support messages are retained only as reasonably needed to respond and maintain support records.

Platform and hosting providers

The app relies on Android and its image picker/camera interfaces and Google ML Kit. These components may be subject to their respective providers' terms and privacy practices. The app does not control Android or Google Play services' independent technical or diagnostic processing.

These policy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about visits to the policy website under GitHub's Privacy Statement. That website hosting is separate from the OCR app; the app does not embed the policy website as an in-app tracking service.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Scan history and preferences remain in the app's local storage until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. You can remove individual history entries or clear all history from the History screen. Temporary image files are used for local processing and are not intended as a permanent image library. Android manages app-private files and their removal. Uninstalling the app normally removes its app-private data, subject to operating-system backup or device settings outside the app's control.

Keshab Studios does not receive or retain your local scan history, preferences, selected images, or OCR text through an app backend. Contact support if you believe you sent personal information to us and want to request its deletion.

Children's privacy

SnapText OCR is intended for people aged 18 or older. It is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information through an app-operated service. If you believe a child has contacted support, email us so we can review and delete the message where appropriate.

Security

Local information is protected by the security features of your device and operating system. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Use a device lock, keep your operating system updated, and avoid sharing sensitive text with apps or people you do not trust.

Your choices and rights

You can decline camera permission, choose not to save or share text, remove history, change preferences, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Depending on your location, applicable privacy law may give you additional rights. To ask a privacy question or make a request about information you sent directly to support, contact keshabstudios@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request and will respond as required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above shows when this version was last revised. Material changes will be reflected on this page; please review it periodically.
